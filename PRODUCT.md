# PRODUCT.md

## Name

T&T Labs

## One sentence

The official website of T&T Labs, a small independent team that makes
developer tools (agent skills), desktop and industrial applications, and a game,
and the single place that links to each of those projects.

## Audience

Pinned from the brief: a developer, player or potential user who heard of one
T&T Labs project (a Roblox Studio skill, VeloBoost, IZDAR, the game) and opened
the company site to see who is behind it and what else they make. They
skim, on desktop or on a phone, and leave through a project link within a
minute. A secondary reader is someone evaluating the team itself (a collaborator,
a teacher, a future employer) who wants the real origin story without spin.

## Primary job of this screen

Find a project and open it: one click from the home page to a GitHub repository,
a product website, or an internal product page.

## Domain materials

The materials belong to this team's actual work, not to "a tech startup":

- The T&T Labs mark: two intersecting discs (a soft grey disc and a copper disc)
  whose overlap reads as an eclipse. It is the only brand asset the team has.
- The celestial horizon from the brand imagery: a planet edge lit by copper light.
- Repository names and paths (`adaybekovt-boop/T-TRS`, `T-T-Founders/izdar-industrial`):
  the real destinations of the Skills division.
- Roblox Studio asset grids, Luau source, Blender meshes, a Claude Code skill folder:
  the objects the four skills operate on.
- Industrial emission logs (the ПЭК journal IZDAR checks), a Windows process
  priority list (VeloBoost), a system hardware readout (MyPC).
- Godot scenes and the game's own art.

## Local / cultural context

Interface language: English only (brief). The team started as college students;
IZDAR's own site is in Russian and serves Kazakhstan industrial reporting, so
place names and product names stay as the products spell them. Density:
editorial for the company story, regular for the project directory.

## Anti-references

1. A freelancer or agency portfolio (case-study grid, "services", client logos)
   because T&T Labs sells nothing as a service and has no clients to show.
2. A generic AI-startup landing (gradient hero, "Get started" CTA, pricing,
   testimonials) because there is no SaaS, no pricing and no customers.
3. A Microsoft-style corporate mega-menu with dozens of links, because there are
   three divisions and eight projects. We borrow the *directory role*, not the scale.

## Constraints from the human

- "Five visual references are the primary visual specification" — cream paper,
  warm copper light, editorial serif headings, dark Games section, celestial hero.
- "The client explicitly requested an Anthropic-inspired visual direction."
- "Every visible sentence must communicate a real fact, explain a project, or
  enable an action." No invented features, numbers, customers or team members.
- Screens: Home (the hero, then the team's story in three screens, over one picture), then Skills, Applications and Games,
  each a separate page reached from the header. The home page never scrolls
  into the divisions.
- Minimal copy: no text that carries no information (eyebrows, genre/engine
  tags, native-name subtitles, decorative captions).
- Skills link straight to GitHub. Applications and Games never link to GitHub
  from the card; they open a real product site or an internal product page.
- English only. No Careers / Research / Press / Investors.

## What we are not building

- No pricing, testimonials, logo walls, metrics, newsletter, contact form.
- No team roster (no verified names were supplied).
- No blog or news section.
- No fabricated domains: VeloBoost, MyPC and the game get internal pages.
- No showreel / play button (there is no video).
